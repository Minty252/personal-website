About

I have run online shops since 2008: first my own, today one for an employer. I know the whole chain from product data and Google Shopping to payment, shipping labels and bookkeeping, and I do most of it with software I write myself.

What I offer

I build and run your online shop at my own cost. You keep the goods, the packing and the customer relationship. I take care of the shop, Google Shopping, advertising, payments and technology, and I get a share of online revenue that we agree up front. No revenue, no fee.

This suits a retailer or manufacturer with a loyal customer base and no shop, or a creator who sells by direct message today.

We start with a small test on your existing customers. Only when that shows people buy online do we build the full shop.
