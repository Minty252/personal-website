Frequently asked questions

What does the site cost?

€60 as an introductory price for the first 5 couples, €99 after that. The web address for the first year is included.

Do we need to know anything about tech?

No. You send me texts and photos, I do the rest. For changes, just message me on WhatsApp.

How long does the site stay online?

Until 3 months after the wedding. That gives your guests time to upload their photos after the party.

Do our guests need an app or an account?

No. They open the link or scan the QR code and reply right on the page.

Who sees our guests' replies?

The guest list is password-protected. Your guests only see their own reply.
